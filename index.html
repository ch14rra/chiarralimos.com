About

Bridging research, design, and the manufacture of medical technology.

I'm a Biomedical Engineering student at UC Irvine researching tumor-suppressor biology in the Kessenbrock Lab at UCI Health's Chao Family Comprehensive Cancer Center, using single-cell RNA sequencing to study how mutant proteins reshape epithelial cells.

Alongside that research, I design and build: leading a team through a low-cost hand orthosis for stroke rehabilitation, and engineering the suspension of an award-winning rescue rover. That combination — lab-based discovery paired with hands-on device design — is what draws me toward manufacturing medical devices and pharmaceuticals, where research has to hold up under real-world production.
